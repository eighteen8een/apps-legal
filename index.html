Padamoji Privacy Policy

Effective date: 27 October 2026 · App: Padamoji (com.arushwi.padamoji)

In short: Padamoji does not collect, share or sell any personal data. There are no accounts, no ads and no tracking. Your progress stays on your own device.

1. Who we are

Padamoji is a Kannada emoji-clue crossword game published by Arushwi Productions. You can reach us at eighteen8een@gmail.com.

2. Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. Specifically, Padamoji does not ask for or access your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, microphone, camera or advertising ID, and it does not require you to create an account.

3. Information stored on your device

To let you continue where you left off, the app saves the following only on your device:

  • your game progress: level, XP, coins, streak, and the puzzle you are currently solving;
  • your settings, such as theme, sound and accessibility options.

This data is never sent to us or to anyone else. It is removed when you uninstall the app or clear the app's storage in your device settings.

4. Internet use

Padamoji works fully offline, and the current version does not send any information from your device. If a future version downloads new word lists, it will only fetch the list of words and will not send any information about you.

5. Advertising and third parties

Padamoji contains no advertising and no analytics or tracking tools, and it does not share data with third parties. The app has no in-app purchases.

6. Sharing

If you tap Share on the results screen, the app prepares a short text message (puzzle size, difficulty, accuracy and emoji) and hands it to the app you choose, such as a messaging app. Nothing is shared unless you choose to share it.

7. Children

Padamoji is a general-audience puzzle game. Because it collects no personal data from anyone, it collects none from children either.

8. Your choices

Since nothing leaves your device, you stay in control: you can delete all of your game data at any time by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update it on this page and change the effective date above. If a change affects how your data is handled, we will tell you inside the app.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy? Email eighteen8een@gmail.com.
